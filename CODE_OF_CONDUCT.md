# Code of Conduct

OpenStreetRacer is a community project. Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and inclusive, and to focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Raise concerns privately through the contact channel listed on the repository host; do not post private or identifying information in a public issue. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and fairly.
